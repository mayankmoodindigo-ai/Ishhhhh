Put your meme video in this folder, named meme.mp4

Then in index.html, find the placeholder block on the last page
and replace it with:
  <video controls playsinline class="media-frame" src="assets/video/meme.mp4"></video>

Using a GIF instead? Put it in assets/images/ instead and use:
  <img src="assets/images/meme.gif" class="media-frame">
