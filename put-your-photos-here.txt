Put your photo files in this folder — e.g. photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg

Then in index.html, find a placeholder block like this:
  <div class="media-placeholder">...</div>

...and replace it with:
  <img src="assets/images/photo1.jpg" alt="a photo" class="media-frame">

(change "photo1.jpg" to whatever you actually named your file)
